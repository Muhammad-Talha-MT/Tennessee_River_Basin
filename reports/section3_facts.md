## 8. Folds

- **Random folds** (`src/tva_flood/data/folds.py: holdout_folds`, called with `splits.RANDOM_FOLDS` = 5, seed 0): eligible gauges are CAMELS-H sites with observations before 2019 and a finite NHDPlus drainage area; they are sorted by drainage area and each consecutive group of 5 gets a random permutation of the fold numbers, so every fold spans small to large basins. 122 gauges are in a fold.
- **Blocked basins** (`src/tva_flood/hydro/splits.py: blocks`): up to 3 disjoint complete upstream basins of CAMELS-H gauges, each holding 8 to 30 scorable gauges (`BLOCK_GAUGES`) and at most 25% of the network area (`BLOCK_AREA`), largest gauge count first. Fold k withholds every catalog gauge inside basin k from training targets, inputs and model selection, and scores the CAMELS-H gauges inside it. Gauges downstream of a block stay in training.

## 9. What a model sees for a withheld gauge

No observed discharge of a withheld gauge, nor of any other withheld gauge, is available at any time: `Samples.gauge_view` sets their flow to missing for the whole window and marks them not visible (src/tva_flood/hydro/samples.py), and fold models never train or select on them (hydro/train.py `withheld_gauges`). Checked by unit tests (tests/test_hydro.py: `test_gauge_view_hides_withheld_gauges_and_shows_only_eligible_ones`, `test_withheld_gauge_observation_never_enters_insertion`) and on real checkpoints (reports/v3_tables/leak_check.csv). Observations of the gauges that are not withheld are available before the issue only, and only at gauges with at least 8 values in the last 32 steps (`Samples.eligible`).

Observed discharge is treated as available up to the issue time (the six-hour mean ending at 00Z is the last value shown); the code models no reporting latency, so a real-time application would see USGS data later than this.

## 14. Blocked basins compared with the other scored gauges

How the blocked basins were chosen: **config and function**, not a commit message or a separate file. `src/tva_flood/hydro/splits.py` sets `BLOCKS` = 3, `BLOCK_GAUGES` = (8, 30) scorable gauges per basin and `BLOCK_AREA` = 0.25 (largest share of the network area in one basin), and `blocks()` takes the eligible CAMELS-H gauges largest first and keeps the first disjoint upstream basins that meet the rules (deterministic). | Mean annual precipitation | AORC six-hour rain, `processed/six_hour_nodes` APCP_surface (mm per six-hour step), 2000-2019 | per reach: finite steps scaled to 1,461 per year, averaged over years; per gauge: area-weighted over the upstream basin; mean over gauges |
| Forest and developed share | StreamCat NLCD 2011 (`static/tennessee_landcover_2011.nc`), watershed (`ws`) values at the gauge reach | forest = mixed + deciduous + coniferous forest; developed = open + low + medium + high intensity; mean over gauges |

Caveats: (1) the AORC units (mm per six-hour step) are assumed from the repository's data notes, not read from a unit attribute; the resulting annual totals (1,160-1,970 mm per gauge basin) are the check. (2) Slope is the reach value, not a catchment slope. (3) Forest and developed shares are NLCD 2011 at the watershed level, which is the upstream watershed of the gauge, so they overlap between nested gauges. (4) Group means are unweighted across gauges, so large and small basins count equally.

Groups: B1-B3 are the scored gauges of each blocked basin (12, 11, 6); 'other' is the rest of the 101 gauged-protocol gauges (72). Each gauge is one unit, so a basin row summarises its gauges, not the basin as one area. Per-gauge values and the group of each gauge: `figures/section3/blocked_vs_other_gauges.csv`; code: `scripts/section3/blocked_vs_rest.py`.

## 16. Open questions / inconsistencies

- Catalog gauges: 128; scored in the gauged protocol: 101; in a random fold: 122; scored held-out: 98. All four counts are used in the repo for different purposes.
- Gauges with training values: `gauges_public/gauges.json` says 123 (any value before the USGS start), `Gauges.trained` counts 122 (more than one year of six-hourly values before 2019), and 122 are in a fold (any value before 2019 and a finite area).
